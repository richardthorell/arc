# ARC Starter Content

The Blank 3D template includes a small material and texture set so newly created projects have useful surfaces in the Content Browser immediately.

## Materials

- Prototype Pattern 01 and 02: textured blocking/debug materials.
- Matte White and Matte Black: neutral rough reference surfaces.
- Brushed Metal: metallic response reference.
- Dark Rubber: high-roughness dielectric reference.
- White Ceramic: glossy dielectric/clear-coat reference.
- Emissive Blue: emissive response reference.
