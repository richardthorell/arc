# ARC Starter Content

The Blank 3D template includes a small material and texture set so newly created projects have useful surfaces in the Content Browser immediately.

## Materials

- Prototype Pattern 01, 02, and 05-08: textured blocking/debug materials with visible scale and orientation cues.
- Matte White and Matte Black: neutral rough reference surfaces.
- Brushed Metal: metallic response reference.
- Dark Rubber: high-roughness dielectric reference.
- White Ceramic: glossy dielectric/clear-coat reference.
- Emissive Blue: emissive response reference.

All material descriptors use ARC's standard `arc/default_phong` surface material schema and asset-root-relative texture paths.

## Third-party textures

The six `textures/starter/kenney_prototype_*.png` files are selected from Kenney's Prototype Textures, repackaged by Calinou for Godot. They are intentionally small default development assets rather than production art.
